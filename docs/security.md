# Security & privacy

- **Stays on your computer.** The Helper only listens on `127.0.0.1` (this
  computer). It is not reachable from your network or the internet.
- **Only PhoneMistri can use it.** Websites other than PhoneMistri are refused,
  and the first connection from a browser must be approved on this computer.
- **Prints, nothing else.** It sends documents to your printers. It does not
  read your files and does not send your data anywhere.
- **Updates** are downloaded from this repository's Releases page.

Found a security problem? Please report it privately through PhoneMistri
support rather than opening a public issue.
